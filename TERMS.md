## Purpose

Gentle Break Reminder is a local productivity utility that uses prompt-submit timing and counts to display configurable break reminders. It is not a background screen-time monitor and does not measure actual work duration, fatigue, hydration, or health status.

## No medical or health advice

Reminders are general wellbeing suggestions only. The plugin is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, emergency assistance, or professional medical advice. Do not rely on it for a medical condition or safety-critical schedule.

## User control and responsibility

You control whether the plugin is enabled, its reminder text, thresholds, delivery channel, cooldown, and daily limit. You are responsible for reviewing the configuration and deciding whether reminders are appropriate for your circumstances.

The plugin checks rules only when Codex submits a supported Hook event. It does not guarantee delivery at an exact time, and reminders may be delayed, suppressed by cooldown or daily limits, or unavailable on unsupported environments.
